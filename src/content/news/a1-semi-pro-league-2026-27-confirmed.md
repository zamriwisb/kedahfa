The Amateur Football League has confirmed a fourteen-team A1 Semi-Pro League for
2026/27, with the season kicking off on 28 August.

Kedah FA are among the fourteen, alongside defending champions Johor Darul
Ta'zim II, Selangor FC II, Perak FA, Negeri Sembilan FC II, UM-Damansara United,
Kelantan City FC, Manjung City FC, Armed Forces FC, Bunga Raya FC, Malaysian
University-UiTM, USM FC, AAK Unisel FC and Imigresen FC II.

## The season ahead

Twelve clubs were confirmed in late July, with AAK Unisel FC and Imigresen FC II
taking the two remaining places to reach the AFL's target of fourteen. The
expanded league runs to twenty-six matchweeks, each side meeting every opponent
home and away.

The league opens on 28 August with Johor Darul Ta'zim II against USM FC. Kedah
begin at home to Imigresen FC II the following night.

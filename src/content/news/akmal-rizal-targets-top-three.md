Akmal Rizal Ahmad Rakhli has set Kedah FA a target of finishing in the top three
of the A1 Semi-Pro League, matching or bettering last season's third place.

"I want better achievements, in line with the KPIs set for me," the head coach
said. "God willing, we will make sure the results are more impressive."

## A largely local squad

Around 90 per cent of the squad is local, weighted towards young players and
balanced by a core of experienced heads. Ghanaian midfielder Anthony Arthur is
the first import through the door, with further additions assessed in defence
and attack.

## Building through pre-season

Kedah have lined up five friendlies before the league begins in late August,
against both amateur and A1 opposition, and have already won the Sri Semantan
Cup 2026 in Kota Bharu.

"The main objective is to see all players in action and following the planned
tactics," Akmal said. "Every player wants to be in the starting eleven."

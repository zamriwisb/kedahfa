Kedah FA have appointed Akmal Rizal Ahmad Rakhli as head coach ahead of the
2026/27 A1 Semi-Pro League season.

Akmal Rizal, who played for Kedah during his career, arrives from Kelantan The
Real Warriors and has also managed Penang. He replaces Victor Andrag, who took
the side to third place in 2025/26 on 57 points from 17 wins, six draws and
five defeats.

## A considered appointment

The Kedah Football Association described the appointment as the outcome of a
thorough search intended to keep the team's direction on track for the new
season.

"The appointment is believed capable of bringing fresh energy to the team while
supporting short and long-term plans," said KFA deputy president Datuk Abdul
Rahman Abdullah.

Baddrol Bakhtiar has been appointed team manager of Kedah FA for the 2026/27 A1
Semi-Pro League season.

The appointment was announced by Kedah Football Association vice-president
Muhammad Radhi Mat Din. Baddrol captained Kedah Darul Aman FC before joining
Sabah FC in the same role, and won the Gurun state seat in 2023.

## Structure and discipline

KFA pointed to his playing and leadership experience as the reason for the
appointment, with the aim of strengthening the squad's structure and discipline
after a third-place finish last season.

He joins a staff that already includes head coach Akmal Rizal Ahmad Rakhli,
appointed earlier in June.
